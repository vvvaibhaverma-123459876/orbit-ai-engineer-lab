# Exceptions, chaining, and recovery

Catch the narrowest exception you can recover from. `else` runs only when the
`try` body succeeds; `finally` is for cleanup and runs on success, failure, or
return. Use `raise NewError(...) from error` to preserve the causal chain while
presenting a domain-level error to callers.

Do not use exceptions for ordinary branching or catch `Exception` at every
layer. Translate at boundaries, log context once, and preserve the original
cause for diagnosis.
